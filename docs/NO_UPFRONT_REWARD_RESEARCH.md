# No-upfront-cost reward research

Last reviewed: 2026-11-18

## Decision rule

This watchlist is restricted to opportunities that do not require buying tokens, depositing funds, paying for a server, purchasing hardware, staking, providing liquidity, or trading to qualify. Existing devices and already-paid connectivity may be considered, but incremental network fees and energy costs must still be disclosed. A token/points balance is not cash income.

For a candidate to be marked actionable, the engine requires current official evidence, explicit country eligibility, known KYC requirements, a verifiable payout mechanism and value, a complete cost basis, and no unapproved capital lockup. If any of these are unknown, the candidate remains blocked.

## Current leads

### Acurast Processor Lite — strongest hardware-fit lead, not yet approved

Official documentation:
- Rewards: https://docs.acurast.com/processors/rewards/
- Provider onboarding: https://docs.acurast.com/processors/become-compute-provider/
- FAQ and network fees: https://docs.acurast.com/faq/
- Legal terms: https://acurast.com/terms-conditions/
- Processor app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acurast-processor/id6517361921

Verified facts:
- Acurast documents a Base Benchmark Rewards pool for compute providers that is independent of participation in Staked Compute.
- The documented network-wide Base Benchmark pool is 856.164 ACU per epoch (900 blocks, roughly 1.5 hours).
- The pool is divided across benchmark metric pools and all competing providers. A phone's share depends on its benchmark score and the aggregate score/competition in the relevant pool.
- Processor Lite is designed for everyday phones. Official docs list iOS 16+ and Android 12+ compatibility; it does not require factory-resetting the everyday phone.
- Staking adds a separate reward pool but is not required for the Base Benchmark pool.
- The FAQ reports network transaction fees typically around 0.0013 ACU per transaction.

Unknown / blockers:
- No official per-device or monthly reward amount is published in the reviewed docs. The 856.164 ACU figure is the network-wide pool, not an individual user's reward.
- Current Russian-resident eligibility has not been explicitly confirmed. General legal terms do not establish RU approval.
- KYC requirements for an ordinary processor reward payout are not confirmed.
- Whether a brand-new processor wallet can cover initial transaction fees without acquiring ACU is not confirmed.
- Electricity, battery wear, network costs and actual device benchmark share have not been measured.

Assessment:
- No hardware purchase and no staking appear necessary for the base pool if an already-owned compatible phone is used.
- This is not yet a verified zero-cash-start opportunity because initial network fee funding and RU eligibility are unresolved.
- Do not buy ACU, rent infrastructure, or use VPN/location evasion to attempt participation.
- Potential per-device income is currently unquantifiable; do not multiply the network-wide pool by token price and call it personal earnings.

## Explicitly excluded

### Grass — excluded for Russian residents

Official rewards terms: https://www.grassfoundation.io/rewards-program-terms-and-conditions

The terms expressly list Russia as a Prohibited Jurisdiction and prohibit VPN use to circumvent geographic restrictions. Excluded from the user's opportunity queue; no participation or claim instructions should be generated.

### Silencio Voice AI — USDC payout excluded for Russia

Official payout terms: https://www.silencio.network/payout-rewards-terms

USDC compensation requires accepted data, customer purchase/approval, KYC and sanctions screening. The terms explicitly say they do not pay persons or comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions including Russia. Excluded from the user's opportunity queue.

### AIOZ DePIN — not a zero-investment candidate yet

Official docs:
- https://docs.aioz.network/aioz-depin/depin-app/depin-cli
- https://docs.aioz.network/aioz-depin/depin-app/gui-cli

The GUI baseline includes at least 50 GB free storage and a stable 20 Mbps connection. Rewards are for verified resource work; the CLI reports task-specific earnings and the minimum withdrawal is 1 AIOZ. No fixed reward rate or expected monthly amount is published. Russian-resident eligibility remains unverified. Do not rent a VPS or buy hardware; only reconsider if already-owned spare infrastructure qualifies and a zero-cost pilot is possible.

## What the engine must report

For each candidate, keep separate fields for:
- network-wide reward pool;
- per-device/user reward (only if actually published or measured);
- token price snapshot and timestamp;
- realized withdrawable amount;
- initial and recurring fees;
- eligibility and KYC;
- payout threshold and lockup;
- incremental electricity, bandwidth and hardware cost.

Never infer personal income from a global emissions pool, points total, referral headline, token price, or maximum bounty.
